# AI assistance and review disclosure

AI systems assisted with proof development, code generation, verification
scripts, manuscript editing, and adversarial review. The user independently
executed the clean-room Mathematica notebook. All Phase-30 review reports are
AI-only and may share implementation context. They are not human review,
independent peer review, or journal refereeing.

The release separates kernel-checked Lean declarations, exact independent-CAS
recalculations, directed numerical corroboration, typed literature inputs,
and prose claims so a reviewer can assign different weights to each channel.
