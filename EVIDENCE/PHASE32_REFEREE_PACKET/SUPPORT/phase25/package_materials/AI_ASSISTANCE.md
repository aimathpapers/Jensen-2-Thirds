# AI assistance and review disclosure

AI systems assisted with proof exploration, Lean formalization, Python and
shell verification, manuscript drafting, package construction, and
adversarial review. The author executed and supplied the clean-room
Mathematica evidence. Every AI review report is retained as an AI artifact,
including unfavorable findings and later dispositions.

The review process used role-specific analytic, algebraic, Lean,
reproducibility, and hostile-falsification tracks. The repaired-candidate
passes are explicitly classified as targeted correlated AI re-reviews. They
must not be described as independent scholarly consensus, human review, or
peer review.

Lean proof terms are checked by Lean's kernel rather than accepted on an AI
reviewer's authority. Exact and interval programs are executable evidence,
but the correctness of their specifications remains a mathematical review
question. The paper's remaining analytic layer is conventional proof text.

The appropriate public description is:

> an extensively checked, partially formalized proof candidate with exact
> independent-CAS corroboration and adversarial AI review, but no human or
> peer review.
